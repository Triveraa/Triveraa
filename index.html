Our Story

Nestled in the heart of the countryside, Triveraa Sonkupi Eco Resort offers a perfect getaway from the chaos of city life. Surrounded by lush greenery, rolling hills, and fresh air, our resort is designed for those who seek peace, comfort, and natural beauty.

Whether you're here for a weekend retreat, a family vacation, or a quiet solo trip, we provide warm hospitality, cozy accommodations, and experiences that bring you closer to nature.
